## About ANNVITRA
Turning Surplus into Smiles. 🌱 <br>

Annvitra is a FoodTech platform which is building a more sustainable and responsible food ecosystem by addressing one of today’s pressing challenges: food waste amid persistent food insecurity. <br>

Every day, surplus food goes to waste because of gaps in planning, storage, and distribution, while communities continue to face challenges accessing adequate nourishment. Annvitra aims to bridge this gap by connecting surplus food with opportunities for redistribution, helping ensure that edible food is valued and put to better use.
<br>
Working at the intersection of food waste management, sustainability, and social impact, we are focused on developing practical solutions that encourage responsible consumption, reduce avoidable food waste, and support communities.<br>

Our vision extends beyond food redistribution. We believe in creating a circular, resource-conscious ecosystem where surplus becomes an opportunity, sustainability drives action, and collective efforts contribute to meaningful change.<br>

At Annvitra, we stand for: <br>

🌍 **Less Waste:** Encouraging food waste reduction and responsible resource use. <br>

🍽️ **Better Access:** Promoting the redistribution of surplus food to where it can make a difference.<br>

♻️ **Sustainability:** Supporting more responsible consumption and resource management.<br>

💚 **Meaningful Impact:** Building solutions that connect communities, resources, and opportunities.<br>

We believe that a more sustainable future begins with better choices and smarter systems.

Partner with ANNVITRA as a food business, farmer, volunteer, community organisation, or individual who shares our vision of reducing waste and improving access to nourishment.
